## Method and Limits

Retain the previous profile in the absence of specific findings; limit corrections to 1–5 points. Do not deduct braking from the final position, degradation from recovered positions, or power from top speed alone. Distinguish load from chassis efficiency and upgrades from engine upgrades. Consider both drivers and specification differences. Qualitative confidence, not statistical range.

The Monza result consulted is still provisional. This profile incorporates information from the weekend: do not use it to retroactively demonstrate the accuracy of the pre-Monza prediction. For a backtest, you need snapshots prior to each race and out-of-sample comparisons.

Forecast compatibility now uses the same average of the ten capacities, weighted by circuit demands, as shown in the circuit profile (forecast weight: 60%, updated on request). Only for missing profiles does the previous fallback remain. Ranking and recent form retain their own factors. Weights are set on demand, not optimized on the results just observed.
